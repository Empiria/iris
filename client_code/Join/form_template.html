Weak connection? Add settings to this page's address and join again, for example ?q=2&rbr=200&trb=200. q lowers your video resolution (0 is 1080p, 1 is 720p, 2 is 360p), rbr caps the video you send to others and trb caps the video you receive, both in kbps. Other video, audio and screen-share settings are accepted; anything else is ignored.
